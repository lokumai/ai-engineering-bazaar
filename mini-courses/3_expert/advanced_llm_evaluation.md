# Advanced LLM Evaluation

*(Placeholder module: a short overview for now, full lesson content is coming soon.)*

Before you train or change a model, you need a way to tell whether it got better. This module will cover the two families of measures: static, objective metrics that compare an answer with a reference, and dynamic, subjective ones that judge whether an answer is faithful and good.

**Topics this module will cover** (issue #46):
- Static and objective metrics: BLEU (n-gram word matching), ROUGE (overlap and recall), METEOR (which penalizes word order), accuracy and precision, and cosine similarity (BERTScore)
- Dynamic and subjective metrics: faithfulness, LLM-as-judge, and human preference
