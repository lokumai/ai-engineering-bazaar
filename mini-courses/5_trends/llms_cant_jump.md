# LLMs Can't Jump

*(Placeholder module: a short overview for now, full lesson content is coming soon.)*

A language model moves through a problem one small step at a time, and it struggles with the kind of leap a person makes when an idea arrives all at once. This module will look at what that limit is, where it shows, and what people are trying to do about it.
