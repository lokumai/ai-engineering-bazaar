# Recursive Self-Improvement

*(Placeholder module: a short overview for now, full lesson content is coming soon.)*

Recursive self-improvement, or RSI, is the idea of an AI system that improves the very system that makes it, so each round starts from a better place. This module will look at what is real today, what is still a claim, and how projects such as AutoHarness approach it.
