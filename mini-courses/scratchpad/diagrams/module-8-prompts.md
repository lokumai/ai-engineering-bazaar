## Prompt A: which neurons light up

> A hand-drawn diagram on a pale yellow lined notepad, thin black ink, in the style of a quick
> whiteboard sketch. It shows a feed-forward neural network of four vertical layers of small
> circles, connected by thin straight lines.
>
> On the left, a small rounded box holds the handwritten words "Solve: 23 - 20 + 6", with an arrow
> from it into the first layer.
>
> Some of the circles are drawn as lit lamps: filled with warm glowing yellow, with short
> radiating strokes around them. These lit circles form a connected path from the input box across
> the layers to the right side. The remaining circles are plain, unfilled, with thin grey
> outlines, and no glow.
>
> Three handwritten labels sit beside the network with short leader lines pointing at groups of
> circles: the word "arithmetic" points at a group of glowing circles, and the words "painting"
> and "physics" each point at a group of plain unlit circles.
>
> On the right, an arrow leaves the last layer into a small rounded box holding the handwritten
> characters "= 9".

## Prompt B: prompt engineering is turning the knobs

> A hand-drawn diagram on a pale yellow lined notepad, thin black ink, in the style of a quick
> whiteboard sketch. It shows a DJ mixer seen from above: a wide rectangular panel with a row of
> six round knobs along the top and three vertical faders below them.
>
> Each knob has a small arc of tick marks around it and a pointer line showing its setting, and
> each is turned to a different position, some near the high end of its arc and some near the low
> end. Under each knob is a handwritten label, reading in order: "role", "detail", "examples",
> "format", "context", "thinking".
>
> A hand reaches in from the lower left and grips one of the knobs, mid-turn.
>
> A label strip along the left edge of the panel carries the handwritten word "PROMPT". On the
> right, an arrow leaves the panel into a small rounded box holding the handwritten word "output".
