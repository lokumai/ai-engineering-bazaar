Bazaar, four palettes

Same design, four colour schemes. The structure, spacing, type and ornament are generated from one template, so the only thing you are choosing on this page is colour. Every hue comes from the Lokum AI logo, sampled from the file: ground #FFF8E9, rose #DC5F3F, orange #E67410, green #415C29.

Each full page also carries the three changes you asked for: a centred content column with gutters clearing both rails, a sidebar that folds away, a completion tick that reads as a filled disc rather than a hairline, and the level you are in enlarged and marked in the sidebar.

The numbers under each card are computed from the tokens, not asserted. Everything on all four clears 4.5:1.
