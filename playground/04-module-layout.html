RAG & Embeddings

FundamentalsModule 3 of 830 min

Retrieval means the model is never changed. Everything happens in the input, which is why a document added this morning can be answered about this afternoon.

The pipeline, end to end

Question→ Embed the question→ Search the vector store→ Take the top four passages→ Put them in the context→ Answer from what is visible

Four passages come back, they go into the context alongside the question, and the model answers from what it can see rather than from what it remembers.

Why it works: the text gets the whole screen and the interface is four icons. Cost: icon-only strips are the least self-explanatory of the three, and a first-time visitor may not realise the module list is there at all. Needs tooltips, and probably a label on first visit.

My read: A for the default, because a permanent module list is the single biggest fix for “I do not know where I am”, and the trimmed rail costs almost nothing. B is the better shape if you decide the outline matters less than the measure. C is a reading mode to offer on top of A, not instead of it.
