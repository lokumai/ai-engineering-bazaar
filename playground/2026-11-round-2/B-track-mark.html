AI Engineering Bazaar Round 2 choices

How does a reader see which track the page is in?

Each option is drawn twice on the same module, once in each track, so the difference is what you compare. None of the four adds a colour: the five level colours already fill the palette, and a sixth hue beside them would read as a sixth level.

  • B1Name in the bar
  • B2Label with the title Recommended
  • B3A second tile band
  • B4Icon beside the title
B1

The track's name in the bar

A quiet field in the bar, next to the language button, says which track you are in. On the real site it would be the switch from question C as well.

  • The same place on every page: module, level list, progress.
  • One element does two jobs, mark and switch.
  • The bar is where readers look least; the title area says nothing.
  • On a phone the bar has no room for words, so it shrinks to an icon.
B2

A label with the title, and the track in the breadcrumb

Recommended

The line under the title starts with the track's name and its icon, and the breadcrumb gets one more step: Home / For Everyone / Fundamentals.

Why: the words sit where the reader is already looking, it costs no colour, and it reads the same on a phone and right to left.

  • Words, not a code to learn. Screen readers get it for free.
  • The breadcrumb makes the track part of the address, which it is.
  • Scroll past the title and the mark is gone (the bar switch from question C still shows it).
  • One more item on the meta line.

The same two pages in the dark theme

B3

A second tile band for the For Everyone track

For Engineers keeps the Iznik band. For Everyone swaps it for a band of sugar dots in the same three colours, and the rail's heading names the track.

  • Seen out of the corner of the eye, on every page, with no reading at all.
  • Uses the band's own colours, so no level colour is borrowed.
  • The band is the site's signature; two bands weaken it.
  • A pattern means nothing until it is learned, so words are still needed somewhere (the rail heading here, and it is hidden on a phone).
B4

An icon in front of the title

A small tile before the title holds the track's icon: code brackets for For Engineers, an open book for For Everyone. The name appears when you point at it.

  • Clear at a glance once known, and the icons can repeat in the catalog and on the home doors.
  • No extra words on the page.
  • An icon on its own has to be learned, and a hover label does not exist on a phone.
  • Two more icons to draw and keep consistent everywhere they appear.
