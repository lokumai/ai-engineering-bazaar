AI Engineering Bazaar Round 2 choices

Which control switches the module you are reading to the other track?

Each mock below works: click the control in its bar. Try it on LLM Fundamentals, which exists in both tracks, and on Tool Calling, whose For Everyone version is not written yet. The mocks assume that on day one For Everyone has three Fundamentals modules written, and that this reader has finished two modules For Engineers and none For Everyone, so the ticks in the rail change with the track.

  • C1Two-segment toggle
  • C2Track menu in the bar Recommended
  • C3One menu for track and language
  • C4A link under the title
C1

A two-segment toggle in the bar

Both track names sit in the bar side by side, and the one you are in is filled. One click moves you to the same module in the other track.

  • Both choices are always visible, so the reader learns the second track exists without opening anything.
  • One click, no menu.
  • The widest of the four: 297px of the bar in this mock, against 166px for C2, and it cannot fit on a phone.
  • A missing version can only be shown as a dimmed segment, and the explanation comes after the click.
C2

A track menu in the bar, like the language menu

Recommended

A button in the bar names the track you are in. It opens a menu with both tracks, the current one ticked, exactly like the language menu next to it.

Why: it names the track and switches it in one place, it is the same shape as the two menus the bar already has, and a row has room to say "not written yet" before the click.

  • Familiar: the same dropdown as Catalog and the language button.
  • The missing case is explained in the menu, and still leads somewhere useful.
  • Two clicks instead of one.
  • The other track is hidden until the menu is opened (the home page choice and the catalog menu show it).

On a phone the button keeps its icon and drops the words; the repository button has to leave the bar to make room

C3

One menu for track and language

The language button becomes a single menu for how you read this module: the track at the top, the language below it.

  • One button in the bar instead of two.
  • Shows how the two interact: in For Everyone, Türkçe and فارسی are listed as not translated yet.
  • Mixes two unrelated choices, so neither has an obvious name for the button.
  • The longest menu, and the one most readers open only for the language.
C4

A link under the title

Nothing new in the bar. A line under the title says the module is also written for the other track, and links to it.

  • Plain words, where the reader is already looking, and nothing to open.
  • Costs no room in the bar, so it is the same on a phone.
  • Goes against the decision that the switch lives in the bar; it would only work as an extra beside C1, C2 or C3.
  • Only on module pages; the level lists and the progress page would need their own way.
