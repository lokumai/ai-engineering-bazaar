Playground, round 2026-11, round 3

Who stands at the gate, and who runs through it?

The home page opens on a white band with the headline and a lede, and a gate below it. The author wants people standing there: women and men, a child, different faces and skin tones, and an AI robot. When the reader scrolls, the robot turns away and escapes through the gate while the others chase it, playfully. Three decisions follow, three options each, every one a working scene.

Everything is drawn with the same hand as the Sipahi and the Bezirgân (the same 220 by 400 grid, the same flat palette, the same idle motion) and stands on the real gate art from src/components/home/street/art/gate.html. The headline and lede are the home page's own words. Seven figures in every scene:

  • Leyla, Mei and Hatice: three women, with a curly puff, a straight dark bob and a headscarf.
  • Kofi and Ivan: two men, one older with a grey beard and deep brown skin, one young with red hair and freckles.
  • Deniz: a boy, drawn smaller, with curls and a wide smile.
  • Bit: the AI robot, with an antenna, an AI panel on its chest and a grille on its back.

The options share one limit: the arch's pavement is drawn below the gate art, but the home page crops it, so people standing at the gate need about 70 pixels of it left showing. The white band has room to stand people only on the top edge of the wall, beside the headline, at about two thirds the size of the figures at the gate.

L2

The cast and its look

Each option has the same seven: three women, two men, a boy and the robot, in six skin tones, with different hair, faces and ages. Their jobs are ordinary ones: nobody is a type, and nobody holds anything that could hurt. They differ in the face, not only in the clothes: Leyla has wide eyes, a broad nose and gold hoops; Mei almond eyes and round glasses (in today's clothes); Hatice a headscarf and a lined, narrower face; Kofi a grey beard and heavy brows; Ivan freckles, blue eyes and a red beard; Deniz an open smile.

Each figure is named under it, as the round-2 characters are. The robot's accessory follows the costume: a fez in L2a and L2c, headphones in L2b.

L2a

Ottoman bazaar dress

Everyone wears the street's costume: robes with sashes, a turban, a fez, a hotoz cap and a headscarf. Only the robot is not from the bazaar, and it wears a fez.

  • The same world as the Sipahi and the Bezirgân further in, so the street reads as one place.
  • The robot is the only modern thing, which makes the joke clear.
  • Seven long robes give similar silhouettes, so the faces and the hats carry the variety.
  • Nobody looks like the reader of an AI course.
L2b

Today's clothes

The same seven in a hoodie, a blazer, a long coat, a cardigan and a flat cap, striped T-shirt and shorts, with headphones on the robot.

  • The reader sees themselves; trousers, coats and shorts make seven different silhouettes.
  • Modern people stand on an Ottoman gate, then the street behind them is all costume.
  • Clothes date faster than robes.
L2c

A mixed street

Recommended

Mei, Kofi and Deniz are in costume; Leyla, Hatice and Ivan are in today's clothes; the robot wears a fez.

A bazaar that is still open: the old trade and the people building AI share one gate.

  • Each group has a woman and a man, so neither costume belongs to one sex.
  • Robes next to trousers gives the lineup the most varied silhouettes of the three.
  • It needs a rule for who wears what; this one is arbitrary, and the author can reassign it.

L3b

Slip between the people, with a comic beat

The robot starts at the left end of the pavement, turns its back, and tiptoes past Kofi and Hatice. It freezes, glances back over its shoulder, and the crowd gasps; then it hops over Deniz and sprints for the gate. The crowd gives chase, and Kofi and Ivan bump into each other at the door.

  • The most character: the robot's face shows once more during the escape, and the hop is the beat.
  • The longest. At real scroll speed the beats pass fast, and the reader may scroll over them.
  • Needs the robot to start away from the gate, so the people stand between it and the door.

The robot flies in, the crowd follows slowly, pointing

A small rotor opens on the robot's antenna. It turns away, lifts off, and glides into the vault. The people on the wall lean and point and bounce; the two on the pavement follow, slowly, one arm out toward the door.
