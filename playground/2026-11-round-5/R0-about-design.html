1I۱01

Generation is free, and the internet is drowning in AI slop.

You ask AI a simple question, and instead of giving you a straight answer, it writes the Treaty of Versailles: pages of polite, robotic text that says everything while explaining nothing. Anyone can generate thousands of words in three seconds, but real understanding has never been harder to find.

2II۲02

AI Engineering Bazaar was born out of frustration with that noise.

We did not want another heavy textbook or a boring 40-hour course. We wanted a simple, honest cheatsheet: something that gives you the key 30% of practical knowledge so you can understand 70% of the topic, with trusted links if you want to explore deeper.

3III۳03

So we made one strict rule: No AI was allowed to write our content.

Why? Because this field changes too fast for AI training data, machines do not understand the real history behind why tools were built, and most importantly, AI has no empathy. A machine cannot remember what it feels like to be confused. Only a human who has struggled with a hard problem knows how to explain it simply to another human.

4IV۴04

Every single topic became a labor of love.

We spent hours deciding what to keep and what to cut. We shared our drafts with real beginners, listened to their feedback, and rewrote everything until it clicked. We searched through hundreds of articles to pick only the best ones, and drew diagrams by hand when words were not enough. We even included Gen-Z style memes to make it enjoyable. AI can do many things we humans do, but it cannot enjoy a good brainrot. That is why.

5V۵05

Why spend so much human effort on something a prompt could generate in seconds?

Because, as Nietzsche once suggested, striving for something that feels nearly impossible is exactly what makes it worth pursuing.

Our motto:

“Imperfect Human Art with Soul. Not a Curriculum.”
