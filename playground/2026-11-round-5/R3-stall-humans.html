3AHumans for Humans2D · SVG One hand remembers its own knot, then passes a lit lamp to another; in its light the second hand's tangle becomes one clean thread.

So we made one strict rule: No AI was allowed to write our content.

Why? Because this field changes too fast for AI training data, machines do not understand the real history behind why tools were built, and most importantly, AI has no empathy. A machine cannot remember what it feels like to be confused. Only a human who has struggled with a hard problem knows how to explain it simply to another human.
