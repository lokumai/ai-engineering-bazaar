Round 5 · About page · stall 4

Every single topic became a labor of love, drawn five ways

Each option is shown as the stall will sit on the dark ground: the drawing on the left in its square slot, the text on the right, revealed as you scroll. Stall 4 is even, so its drawing takes the left; stalls 3 and 5 put theirs on the right.

  • 4A is the author's idea: a cherub with a bow and a heart-tipped arrow, in the engraving spirit of the old "Labor of Love" covers, drawn new here.
  • 4B is three.js, a real 3D scene. 4D is CSS 3D: a sheet tilted on a desk, which tilts further as you scroll.
  • Each starts when it comes into view and pauses when it leaves. With reduced motion, or with ?still on the address, each shows one still frame that tells the whole story.
4A

The cherub's arrow

2D · SVG

A winged cherub looses a heart-tipped arrow; where it lands, a crossed-out draft becomes a clean page, and hearts rise from it.

04

Every single topic became a labor of love.

We spent hours deciding what to keep and what to cut. We shared our drafts with real beginners, listened to their feedback, and rewrote everything until it clicked. We searched through hundreds of articles to pick only the best ones, and drew diagrams by hand when words were not enough. We even included Gen-Z style memes to make it enjoyable. AI can do many things we humans do, but it cannot enjoy a good brainrot. That is why.
