Why strive for the nearly impossible

Five drawings for the last stall. 5A is the author's idea; 5B to 5E are proposals. Each one sits as the stall will: its text on one side, its drawing in the square slot on the other. It plays when it reaches the screen, and Replay plays it again. The motto that closes the page is at the bottom.

5ANietzsche, engraved2D · SVG The author's idea: an original portrait of Nietzsche, inked line by line on an engraving plate, his gaze lifting as the light crosses his face.
V

Why spend so much human effort on something a prompt could generate in seconds?

Because, as Nietzsche once suggested, striving for something that feels nearly impossible is exactly what makes it worth pursuing.
